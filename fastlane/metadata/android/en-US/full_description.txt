<p>LOR is a to-do app that stays on your phone and won't let you forget. There is no account, no cloud and no internet permission: every task lives on your device and nowhere else.</p>

<p><b>Reminders that work</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Exact notifications with Done, Snooze 10 min and Snooze 1 hr buttons</li>
<li>Full-screen alarms that ring over the lock screen with your choice of sound</li>
<li>Nudges that keep coming at an interval you set until the task is done</li>
<li>Optional morning and evening summaries</li>
<li>Reminders survive reboots and app updates</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Tasks your way</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Notes, due date and time, Low/Medium/High priority, custom lists and subtasks</li>
<li>Repeat daily, weekly, monthly or on chosen days</li>
<li>Swipe right to finish, swipe left to delete, with Undo</li>
<li>Search, filter and sort</li>
</ul>

<p><b>See your day</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Today view with overdue tasks highlighted and upcoming tasks</li>
<li>Calendar, completed history, weekly count and streak</li>
<li>Water tracker and daily rings</li>
<li>Home screen widget with checkboxes</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Your data stays yours</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Export and restore everything as a JSON file you control</li>
<li>Delete everything at any time</li>
<li>No ads, trackers or analytics. Free software under the GPL-3.0.</li>
</ul>
